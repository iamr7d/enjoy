# Credits, licensing and what is withheld

## Film material

The hero image on the project page is generated from **Sintel** (2010), the Blender Foundation
open movie, licensed **CC BY 3.0**. The four panels are one real frame of it passed through the
viewer's four stages: the source frame, the same frame foveated at the viewer's fixation, the
density it samples from, and where it landed.

> *Sintel* © copyright Blender Foundation | durian.blender.org

## Withheld from this public copy

Two figures reproduce frames from broadcast documentary footage in the DIEM film corpus. The
thesis reproduces frames only where a figure requires them; publishing them on a public website
goes past that, so in this copy they are replaced by a notice. Both appear in the submitted
thesis.

- `maps_example.png` — two frames through the whole pipeline
- `fig09_scanpaths.png` — generated scanpaths over two clips

## Datasets

Neither corpus is redistributed here.

- **DIEM**, film viewing with eye-tracking. Mital, P. K., Smith, T. J., Hill, R. L. and
  Henderson, J. M. (2011) 'Clustering of gaze during dynamic scene viewing is predicted by
  motion'. *Cognitive Computation* 3(1), 5–24.
- **COCO-Search18**, task-directed visual search. Chen, Y., Yang, Z., Ahn, S., Samaras, D.,
  Hoai, M. and Zelinsky, G. (2021) 'COCO-Search18 fixation dataset for predicting goal-directed
  attention control'. *Scientific Reports* 11, 8776.

## Models

The bottom-up saliency model and the vision-language model are third-party pretrained systems,
used unmodified and not redistributed.
